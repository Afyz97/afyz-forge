# Copy patterns by element

## Buttons and CTAs (B1, CM5)

Open with a verb and name the result: "Start free trial", "Save changes", "Create account".
"Submit", "OK" and "Click here" say nothing. The label must describe what actually happens
when it is pressed.

## Errors (ER2)

Three parts: **what went wrong + why (when it helps) + what to do now.**

> Payment didn't go through. Your bank declined the card. Use another card or contact your bank.

Speak in the interface's normal voice. No apology unless the system itself failed (ER4), no
blame, never vague, never only a code. "Empathy" here means being clear and useful, not sorry.

## Empty states (O1)

What belongs here + why it is empty + how to begin.

> No projects yet. Create one to start working with your team.

An empty screen is an invitation; give it one clear action. First use, no search results and
"everything cleared" are different situations with different copy.

## Confirmation dialogs (B4)

- Title names the action and the object: "Delete 3 files?" (never "Are you sure?").
- Body states the consequence: "This can't be undone."
- Buttons repeat the action: "Delete files" / "Keep files" (never OK / Cancel or Yes / No).

## Tooltips

Short and useful; never restate what the label already says. Essential information never lives
only in a tooltip (hover is invisible on touch).

## Loading

Say what is happening and, for long waits, how long (FP1). Calm the user; do not fill the wait
with jokes about their money or data (CM8).

## Onboarding (O2)

Introduce one idea at a time, at the moment it matters. A tour does not rescue an unclear
screen (O3).

## Tone by moment

| Moment | Tone |
|---|---|
| Success | Pleased, not over the top |
| Error | Direct and helpful |
| Warning | Clear about the risk and the action |
| Neutral | Informative, brief |
