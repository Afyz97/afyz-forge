# Interview / session guide (about 50 minutes)

| Part | Minutes | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Welcome | 5 | Build rapport, explain how the session works and consent |
| 2. Their world | 10 | How they do the job today, tools, workarounds |
| 3. Focus topic | 20 | The research question in depth; ask for stories, not opinions |
| 4. Reactions | 10 | Show concepts or a prototype; observe before asking |
| 5. Close | 5 | Anything we missed? Thank them; explain next steps |

Guide contents: the questions for each part, follow-up probes ("Tell me about the last time
that happened", "What did you do next?"), and any activities or tasks with their success
criteria. Avoid leading questions and hypotheticals ("Would you use...").
